Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What we collect

  • Shop orders: your name, phone number, email, delivery address, the products you choose, and the photo(s) you upload for printing, including how you cropped them.
  • Quotation and booking forms: your name, phone, email, event details and any message you write.

Why we use it

We use your details only to make and deliver your order, to reply to your enquiry, and to keep records of what you bought. We do not sell your information or use your photos for anything other than your order.

Your photos

Your photo stays on your device while you design your product. It is uploaded to private storage only when you click Pay, so we can print it. Photos are not public and are only opened by VK Photography to produce your order. We delete order photos 90 days after delivery unless you ask us to keep them or remove them sooner.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank details. We only receive confirmation that your payment succeeded.

Who handles your data

We use Supabase to store orders and photos, Netlify to host this website, and Razorpay for payments. These providers process data on our behalf to run the shop.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by writing to vkphotography263@gmail.com or calling +91 72070 46157.
