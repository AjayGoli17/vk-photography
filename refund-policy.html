Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Our shop products are personalised and made specially from your photo, so they cannot be resold. Please check your photo, crop, size and frame carefully before paying. This policy explains when you can cancel, and what we do if something goes wrong.

Cancelling an order

  • Before we start printing: you can cancel for a full refund. Contact us as soon as possible, ideally within 12 hours of ordering, by WhatsApp or phone with your order number.
  • After printing or framing has started: we cannot cancel or refund, because the product has been made for you.
  • If an order has already been shipped, it cannot be cancelled.

Returns

Because every item is custom-made, we do not accept returns or exchanges for change of mind, or for differences caused by the photo you supplied (for example low resolution, blur, or your chosen crop).

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, has a printing defect, or is not what you ordered, we will replace it free of charge or refund you, whichever you prefer. To make a claim:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery on WhatsApp +91 72070 46157 or at vkphotography263@gmail.com.
  • Send your order number and clear photos of the item and the packaging. A short unboxing video helps if you have one.
  • We will review your claim and reply within 2 working days.

How refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card, netbanking or wallet).
  • We start the refund within 3 working days of approval. It usually reaches your account in 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.
  • The shipping fee is refunded only when we cancel the order, or when the problem is our fault.

Payment problems

If money was deducted but you did not get an order confirmation, wait a short while first. Payments that are not confirmed are normally reversed automatically within 5–7 working days. If yours is not, contact us with your payment reference and we will help.

Photography and video bookings

Advance payments and cancellation or rescheduling terms for events are agreed with you when the booking is confirmed, and apply to that booking.
