Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy covers delivery of products ordered from the VK Photography online shop.

Where we deliver

We deliver across India to any address that a courier can reach. We do not currently ship outside India.

Shipping fee

A flat shipping fee of ₹199 is added to every order, however many items it contains. The fee is shown at checkout before you pay.

How long it takes

  • Making your order: 3–5 working days after payment is confirmed, as each item is printed and prepared specially for you.
  • Courier delivery: usually 3–7 working days after dispatch, depending on your location.
  • Total: most orders arrive in about 6–12 working days from the date of payment.

These are estimates, not guarantees. Festival seasons, weather, remote locations and courier delays can add time. If we expect a delay, we will let you know.

Tracking your order

When your order is dispatched we send you the courier name and tracking number by WhatsApp or email, using the phone number and email you gave at checkout. You can also contact us any time with your order number for an update.

Delivery address

Please check your address, pincode and phone number before paying. We are not responsible for delays or non-delivery caused by an incorrect or incomplete address. If a parcel comes back to us because of a wrong address, you may need to pay the shipping fee again for us to resend it.

Receiving your order

Please open the parcel soon after it arrives. If it is damaged or the wrong item, follow the steps in our Refund & Cancellation Policy, and contact us within 48 hours of delivery.
